## ✨ Key features

### 🔒 Secure storage
The app saves your copy history in a vault. It holds up to 200 items. This history stays on your machine. No cloud servers touch your personal information.

### 📱 Local network sync
Connect your phone and computer to the same Wi-Fi network. The app finds your other devices automatically. It pushes clipboard items between them in seconds.

### 👻 Ghost Layer
Do you copy passwords or sensitive info? Use the Ghost Layer tool. It deletes these items after a set time. You control how long sensitive data stays in your history.

### 🛠 Content transformations
Change your text after you copy it. The app includes 37 tools. These tools format lists, remove line breaks, or change case. Select the text in the app and click a transformation icon to run it.

## 🚀 Setting up the app

The first time you open ClipSync Nexus, the settings window appears. You can pick your preferences here.

1. **Auto-start**: Enable this if you want the app to run when you turn on your computer.
2. **Device Name**: Assign a name to your computer. This makes it easy to spot when you sync your phone.
3. **Ghost Layer rules**: Set the time limit for automatic deletion of private data. You can turn this off if you prefer to manage items yourself.

## 🔄 Using the sync tool

Follow these steps to link your phone and your computer:

1. Install the app on both devices.
2. Verify both devices use the same wireless network.
3. Open the Sync tab in the app on your computer.
4. Open the Sync tab on your Android device.
5. Select your computer name from the list.
6. Confirm the request on your computer screen.

Your clipboard history now shares data between devices. When you copy text on one device, it arrives on the other device shortly after.

## 🛡 Privacy and security

Privacy matters. ClipSync Nexus relies on local connections. Your data travels through your own router. It does not go to external servers. Your history stays inside your hardware. We use standard encryption methods to lock your database file. Even if someone gains access to your computer, they cannot read your clipboard history without the master password you set during the first run.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

**The app does not see my other device.**
Check your Wi-Fi connection. Both devices must share the same network. Ensure your computer firewall allows the app to communicate over the local network. 

**I cannot find the download.**
Check your browser download folder. If the file block triggered by Windows occurs, click "More info" and then "Run anyway." 

**The app runs slowly.**
Check your available memory. If you have many open programs, close them to free up resources. The app requires minimal processing power but benefits from extra memory when syncing large amounts of data.

**How do I clear my history?**
Open the settings menu. Find the History tab. You can clear the entire list or delete specific items one by one. The Ghost Layer also clears items based on your set rules.
